Good to Know

Brandon content FAQ.

Are you a Brandon-based wedding content creator?

Yes. Roam Content Co. is based right here in Brandon, Manitoba. We create content throughout the Wheat City and all of the Westman region, with no travel fee within Brandon.

Which Brandon-area venues do you create content at?

We regularly work at the Riverbank Discovery Centre, the Keystone Centre, the Victoria Inn, the Dome Building downtown, and out at Clear Lake and Elkhorn Resort in Riding Mountain National Park.

How fast will we get our Brandon content?

Fast turnaround is our signature — all of your raw footage the same day, and your polished reels the next day.

Do you cover events beyond weddings in Brandon?

Yes. Beyond weddings we cover conferences at the Keystone Centre, sporting events, festivals like the Westman Lip Sync, and corporate functions across Brandon and Westman.
