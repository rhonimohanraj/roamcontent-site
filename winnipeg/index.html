Good to Know

Winnipeg content FAQ.

Do you create wedding content in Winnipeg?

Yes. We regularly roam Winnipeg weddings and events, from The Forks and Assiniboine Park to venues across the Exchange District and beyond. We’re based in Brandon and travel to Winnipeg often.

Which Winnipeg venues do you work at?

We create content at The Forks, Assiniboine Park and the Qualico Family Centre, Hotel Fort Garry, the RBC Convention Centre, Exchange District lofts, and many private estates across the city.

Can you cover our Winnipeg conference or corporate event?

Definitely. We cover conferences, galas and brand activations at the RBC Convention Centre and venues across Winnipeg, turning the day into recap reels and social assets your team can post the same week.

How fast will we get our Winnipeg content?

Same-day social edits are our signature — a teaser reel before the night ends, with the full gallery and highlight film to follow, typically within 48 hours to two weeks.
