Good to Know

Regina content FAQ.

Do you create wedding content in Regina?

Yes. We roam Regina weddings and events across the Queen City and southern Saskatchewan — from Wascana Park to downtown venues. We travel to Regina regularly from our Manitoba base.

Which Regina venues do you work at?

We create content at Wascana Park and Lake, the Conexus Arts Centre, Hotel Saskatchewan, Government House, and venues throughout Regina and the surrounding area.

Can you cover sporting and live events in Regina?

Absolutely — live events are a specialty. Game day at Mosaic Stadium, tournaments, festivals and concerts: we shoot high-energy content in the thick of it and edit it for the moment.

How fast will we get our Regina content?

Same-day social edits are our signature — a teaser reel before the night ends, with the full gallery and highlight film to follow, typically within 48 hours to two weeks.
