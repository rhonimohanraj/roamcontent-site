Good to Know

Frequently asked questions.

What is a wedding content creator, and how is it different from a videographer?

A content creator is your invisible guest with a camera — capturing candid, social-first clips and photos on the move and delivering them fast, often the same night or within 48 hours, so you can share the moment while it’s still happening. It complements (rather than replaces) a traditional photographer or cinematographer: think behind-the-scenes, getting-ready, and the in-between moments, edited natively for Instagram, TikTok and Reels.

Where are you based, and where do you travel?

We’re based in Brandon, Manitoba, and we create content across Brandon, Westman, Winnipeg, Regina, and all of Manitoba and Saskatchewan — plus destination weddings and events on request.

What do you shoot on?

Our content is captured natively on the latest iPhone Pro with professional gimbals, lenses and lighting — that’s what makes it feel real, immediate and platform-ready, with a touch of Super-8 warmth. For highlight films and galleries we bring pro mirrorless gear too.

How fast do we get our content?

Same-day social edits are our signature: a teaser posted before the night is over, your reels within 48 hours, and a full highlight edit to follow within two weeks.

Do you make wedding reels and recap videos?

Yes — wedding reels and recap videos are our core deliverables. You get a same-day teaser reel posted before the send-off, a set of polished vertical wedding reels cut to trending audio within 48 hours, and a full recap video (highlight edit) within two weeks. For conferences and live events we deliver same-week recap reels your team can post right away.

Do you serve the whole Prairie region?

Yes. We’re a Prairie content-creation house based in Brandon, Manitoba, creating wedding and event content across the Canadian Prairies — Brandon and Westman, Winnipeg, Regina, and all of Manitoba and Saskatchewan — plus destination weddings on request.

How do we check our date and book?

Head to our inquiry page with your date, location and what you’re celebrating, and we’ll be in touch within 48 hours to talk through the right collection for you.
